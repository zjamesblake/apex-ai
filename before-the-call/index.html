Before the Call

Thirty minutes with an AI interviewer. It hands you a brief. The brief is what makes the hour worth having.

How this works

  1. Open Claude or ChatGPT and start a new chat. Either works. Use whichever you already have.
  2. Have your calendar and your inboxes open for the last two weeks. Email, Slack, Discord, wherever the asks come in. The interviewer will ask you to walk through them, and memory is not good enough for this part.
  3. Copy the prompt below with the button and paste it in as your first message. Then answer its questions, one at a time. Talk rather than type. Turn on voice-to-text and brain dump. Say more than you think you need to, flesh the idea out, talk it through, go on tangents. Messy is better than tidy here, because the more you give it, the more it finds. It takes about thirty minutes and it will not accept one-word answers, which is the point.
  4. At the end it hands you a brief, as a file to download or as one message. Reply to the message this link came in and attach the file, or paste the whole brief in. Zak reads it himself, then sends you the booking link.
Do not paste customer data, passwords, or anything you would not put in an email to a new advisor. Revenue bands are enough. If you were not with us in St. Tropez, there is an optional ten-minute read that explains the idea behind all of this: Where You Stand. The interview does not depend on it.
